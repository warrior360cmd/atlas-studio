What we do not promise

No “exactly once” promise without a mechanism your tools guarantee. No automatic payments, sensitive decisions or overhaul of your systems. The prototype is not a production install; nothing depends on a laptop or notebook left running.

14-day feedback window after delivery. Acknowledgement within 2 business days (UTC). Our mistakes are fixed at no cost; a new target or a new request gets a separate quote.

AI and human review

AI models help prepare drafts. Every delivered item is checked by a person, and fact, amount and file checks run as deterministic scripts — not by AI. Your project data is not sent to any third-party AI service.

FAQ

Do we need AI?

Rarely. Deterministic rules come first; AI is added only where it shows a real benefit.

What if a tool goes down?

The item waits, retries at most 3 times, then lands in the exception queue with the failed step and the error.

Who owns the automation?

You do: code or configuration, versions and guide are handed over; it runs in an environment in your name.

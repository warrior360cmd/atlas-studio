Privacy

Data controller: Sandrine Kadjo, sole trader (ATLAS Studio), 4 chemin des roses, 78460 Chevreuse, France. Contact: support form.

Data we process

What you enter (name, email, company, country, brief, messages) and documents you upload to your project. A one-way hash of your IP address is used only to limit abuse (5 briefs per hour). No cookies, no analytics, no advertising.

Purposes and legal basis

Answering your request, quoting, performing and delivering the service (pre-contract steps and contract performance); keeping accounting records (legal obligation).

Technical providers

  • Supabase — database, file storage and server functions, hosted in the Paris region (eu-west-3).
  • Vercel and GitHub Pages — hosting of public pages (no form data is stored there; these hosts may log visitor IP addresses). GitHub Actions also triggers scheduled jobs, with no access to project content.
  • Kaggle (Google) — GPU computing to prepare drafts for paid projects: the brief and relevant files are processed there by a model running locally on the machine, in a private workspace, and erased at the end of the session; only the project reference and status are kept in the log. No draft is delivered without human review.
  • Stripe — card payments; we never see or store card numbers.

Access to your project

Your project page is reached through a secret link given when you send the brief. The secret is stored as a hash; anyone holding the link can read the project, so keep it private. Files download through temporary links (1 hour).

Retention

Briefs not followed by an order: deleted 6 months after the last exchange. Project files: kept 12 months after closure, then deleted, except accounting records kept for the legal period. Earlier deletion on request via support.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, via the support form. You may contact the competent data protection authority.

FAQ

When does e-invoicing become mandatory?

Receiving is mandatory for every VAT-registered business since 1 September 2026; issuing is mandatory since then for large and mid-sized companies, and from 1 September 2027 for SMEs and micro-businesses.

Is a PDF sent by email still valid?

Between VAT-registered businesses in France, a plain PDF will no longer do once the issuing obligation applies: the invoice must be issued in a structured format (Factur-X, UBL or CII) through an approved platform.

What is Factur-X?

A readable PDF that also carries a structured XML file: machines read the XML, people read the PDF. It is one of the three formats accepted by the reform.

Does the free tool send my invoices anywhere?

No. The tool reads the file in your browser; only the SIREN is checked live in the public company register.

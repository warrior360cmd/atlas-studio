What we do not promise

No promise of SEO ranking, conversion or sales. We invent no product attribute: missing information stays visible and blocks that page. “Format validated” and “import tested on your shop” are distinct; import is only tested if you give us access to a test environment.

14-day feedback window after delivery. Acknowledgement within 2 business days (UTC). Our mistakes are fixed at no cost; a new target or a new request gets a separate quote.

AI and human review

AI models help prepare drafts. Every delivered item is checked by a person, and fact, amount and file checks run as deterministic scripts — not by AI. Your project data is not sent to any third-party AI service.

FAQ

Does a variant count as a product?

No: the package covers 30 parent products and up to 60 SKUs (variants). Beyond that, a quote.

What if my data contradicts itself?

We ask you and record your decision before writing. We never decide alone.

Who reviews the English?

Every page gets a human review; if your market needs specialised expertise (regulatory, technical), we say so before quoting.

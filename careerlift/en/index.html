What you receive

  • Fact matrix: employers, dates, roles, education, results — with their origin (document or your statement)
  • Mapping to the posting: requirement, evidence, chosen wording, gap
  • French and English CVs that match (same dates, titles, numbers), language levels never inflated
  • Editable Word files + PDF with selectable text and checked reading order
  • FR/EN professional profile
  • One cover letter or message for the agreed posting (never sent automatically)
  • Open questions and correction log

Options, quoted separately. Extra application, letter translation, a new career direction, rebuilding a complex history, interview preparation based on your real experience.

How an order runs

  1. Online brief + upload your documents to the project page
  2. Fact matrix and questions
  3. Quote and payment

  5. You confirm the facts and send feedback

What we need from you

  • Your current CV and/or career notes
  • The target posting (link or text)
  • Useful diplomas and references (no ID documents)
  • Your constraints (market, language, mobility)

What we do not promise

No interview or job guaranteed, no “ATS score”. We invent no degree, employer, skill, number or language level: a gap becomes a question. Your own statements are never presented as employer-verified.

14-day feedback window after delivery. Acknowledgement within 2 business days (UTC). Our mistakes are fixed at no cost; a new target or a new request gets a separate quote.

AI and human review

AI models help prepare drafts. Every delivered item is checked by a person, and fact, amount and file checks run as deterministic scripts — not by AI. Your project data is not sent to any third-party AI service.

Card payment via Stripe, only after your brief is reviewed and a quote is posted in your project page. Nothing is charged when you send a brief.

FAQ

Yes: you get the Word files and a guide to adapt them yourself.

Do you send the application?

No. We never log into your accounts or send anything on your behalf.

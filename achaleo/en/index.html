What we do not promise

We never submit a bid for you and guarantee no award. We do not set your price. Notices come from the official BOAMP: a notice missing from BOAMP is outside the basic watch. No reference or certificate is ever invented.

AI and human review

AI models help prepare drafts. Every delivered item is checked by a person, and fact, amount and file checks run as deterministic scripts — not by AI. Your project data is not sent to any third-party AI service.

FAQ

Where do the notices come from?

From BOAMP, the official French public procurement bulletin, published as open data by the State. Every notice in our digests links to its official page.

Who submits the bid?

You do. We prepare and review the file; signing and submitting on the buyer’s platform remain your responsibility.

Can a small business win a public contract?

Yes, many contracts are open to small businesses, often split into lots. The watch exists to find the ones that fit your size and references.

Does AI write the technical proposal?

AI may draft an outline, but every claim must match a real reference you provide. A person reviews everything before delivery.

AI and human review

FAQ

Where does the information come from?

From BODACC, the official French bulletin of civil and commercial notices, and the national company register, both published as open data by the State. Every signal links to its official notice.

What is the deadline to file a claim?

Two months from the publication of the opening judgment in BODACC, or four months if you are established outside mainland France. After that the claim is in principle barred from the proceeding, unless a hard-to-obtain relief is granted.

Who sends the claim?

You do. We prepare the draft from your invoice; you sign it and send it to the insolvency practitioner or the official online portal.

Does AI decide the ranking?

No. Ranking follows written rules applied to official notices: an open proceeding is red whatever an AI thinks. A person reviews every report.
